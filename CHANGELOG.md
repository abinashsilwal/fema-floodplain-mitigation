# Changelog

## 2026-10-22
- Project-site analyses restricted to residential buildings; site inventories also list utility lines and
  commercial structures that are not comparable with insured residential units.
- Repeated listings of the same site across subapplications matched on recorded attributes; records within one
  subapplication are never merged. Added a record flow table and alternative fingerprints.
- Signed (asinh) transformation of net development change as a sensitivity check.
- Approval model for fiscal years 2003-2023.

## 2026-10-21
- Within-county comparisons: Mantel-Haenszel claim-rate ratio by county; construction-year shares within counties
  with project sites; ZIP-code models with county fixed effects.
- Direct contrasts between average marginal effects; Conley spatial standard errors.
- Construction year of building sites in funded HMA projects (FEMA GO site inventories, FY2020-2025).
- Applications versus awards; pre-disaster programs; never-applied model.
- Area-weighted EAD crosswalk; NLCD gross versus net change check.
- Other FEMA funding sources (Public Assistance Section 406, NFIP ICC).
- Flood-related declarations include hurricane, tropical storm and coastal storm incidents.

## 2026-10-20
- National NFHL x Annual NLCD run (2,640 deliveries) after a 40-county pilot.
- HMA Subapplications v2 adopted after HMA Projects v4 was withdrawn.
- Analysis plan written before national results were examined.
